# AKS — Brand Foundation & Voice

The soul of the site. Everything else — the AI, the 3D, the checkout — is machinery. This is the reason any of it exists. Read this before writing a single line of copy for AKS. Hand it to Claude Code and to any copywriter as the authority on what AKS *means* and how it *speaks*.

**Launch product (ADR-0015):** soft launch is **ready-to-wear** — standard sizes XS–XXL. Made-to-measure is a later phase, not the storefront default.

---

## 1. The one idea

AKS has two unique selling points, and they are secretly the same idea:

1. **Cut and cloth over noise.** Value is silhouette, fabric, drape, and finishing — not embroidery piles or logos. Ready-to-wear pieces graded with care, not chart theatre.
2. **Made between two worlds.** Eastern craft and soul, a Western quietness of line. Modest by intention, not apology.

Both are about **fit**. One is fit to how the piece hangs on a real body (honest sizes + a clear chart). The other is fit to her *two worlds* — the East she comes from and the West she lives in. AKS says she doesn't have to choose.

**The line everything collapses into:**

> **Cut for how you move. Rooted in both worlds.**

Or, using the name (*aks* = reflection, in Urdu and Persian):

> **A dress that reflects you.**

The name already carries the whole brand in two languages. You rarely have to explain it — you let people notice.

---

## 2. Who she is

One woman, seen from a few angles. Don't write for a "segment" — write for her:

- She might be **abroad** — Pakistani or South Asian, living in London, Toronto, Dubai, New York — dressing between a culture she carries and one she lives in.
- She might be **home in Pakistan**, wanting an Eastern piece with a cleaner, more modern cut.
- She might simply be **any woman tired of clothes that don't hang right** — because that part isn't cultural, it's universal.

What they share: **she wants something that feels like her — quiet, considered, and true to the cloth.** Write to that.

---

## 3. The voice — a friend who sews

Warm, honest, a little witty, never corporate. A friend who sews doesn't *sell* you a dress — she tells you the truth about the cut and then helps you choose the size that will sit well. That's the whole voice.

**Principles**

- **Warm through honesty, not adjectives.** Warmth comes from telling the truth plainly — not from "crafted with love." A friend says "if you're between sizes, size up for a looser line" and means it.
- **Second person, always.** You, your, yours. It's a conversation, not a broadcast.
- **Specific beats poetic.** "Ready to wear — timing depends on the piece" moves her more than "lovingly crafted over time." Detail *is* the emotion.
- **A little wit, lightly.** One dry, human aside is worth more than three exclamation marks.
- **Plain is a flex.** You have a real story, so you can afford to drop the marketing words. Brands reach for "luxury" and "elevated" when they have nothing true to say.
- **Confident, not boastful.** State what you do and stop.
- **Modesty in the tone, too.** Calm and uncrowded, the way the clothes are.

**Never say:** luxury, elevated, curated, crafted with love, timeless elegance, for the modern woman, empower(ed), effortless, must-have, unlock, journey, "we're so excited," exclamation-mark enthusiasm, or anything you'd find on a thousand other stores. If a line could belong to any brand, it isn't AKS.

**The test for every line:** *Would a warm, honest friend who sews actually say this out loud?* If it sounds like a marketing team, rewrite it.

---

## 4. Language — how much Urdu, and where (per-surface recommendation)

**Urdu and Eastern words are seasoning, not a second menu.** Use them where they add soul; never where they cost clarity on a control. On the Pakistan (`ur`) locale everything is fully Urdu (human-written); the guidance below is for the default English experience.

| Surface | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| **Logo / wordmark** | Bilingual: `aks` + عکس together. This lockup *is* the East/West thesis. |
| **Home hero** | English, with the meaning of *aks* revealed once, gently ("*aks* — a reflection"). |
| **Atelier / about page** | Richest place for Eastern language. *Karigar*, *adda*, *aks*. Gloss lightly. |
| **Product & size controls** | English (and `ur` on the Urdu locale). Function first — size labels stay clear. |
| **Order status / production** | Real workshop language when stages are real — "cutting," "with the karigar," "final check." Don't invent MTM stages the shop doesn't run. |
| **Empty & error states** | English, warm, plain. |
| **Fabric library** | Eastern fabric names as they are — lawn, jamawar, khaddar, organza. |
| **Footer / packaging / labels** | Bilingual marks and a single Urdu line (e.g. عکس). |

Rule: an Urdu word appears because it's the *truest* word, never as decoration.

## 5. The copy deck — real lines for the actual screens

Starting points in the AKS voice. Soft launch = **ready to wear**. Adjust, don't sanitize.

**Home — hero**
> Ready-to-wear pieces in natural cloth — Eastern silhouettes, a quieter line.
> Chosen for how they drape, and how they last.

**Home — the idea, one scroll down**
> *aks* means a reflection.
> Eastern hands, a Western calm. Clothes that sit between the two worlds you live in.

**Home — how it works (three plain steps)**
> Choose a look. Pick your size. We pack it and send it — no measurement form, no guesswork.

**Collection / listing intro**
> Looks, not a noisy catalogue. Pick what's yours and we'll send it.

**Product page — size**
> Choose your size — XS to XXL. Between two? Size up for a relaxed line, or message us and we'll help you choose.

**Size guide**
> Here's how each house size measures up. Use the chart, then trust how you like clothes to sit.

**Try it on (Reflection)** — if offered
> See it on you, near enough. Treat this as a good first look at the drape.

**Add to cart / cart empty**
> Empty for now. When you choose a piece, it lives here until checkout.

**Cart — lead time, told honestly**
> Ready to wear — timing depends on the piece. We'll tell you plainly on the bag.

**Checkout — payment, in plain words**
> Pay in full now, or pay part on delivery where we offer it. No half-now / half-later deposit fiction for standard sizes.

**Order placed**
> We have your order. Track it any time — we'll keep you posted as it moves.

**Order status — stages that match the shop**
> Confirmed → Cutting → Stitching → Final check → On its way  
> (Use only stages the production board actually runs.)

**Atelier / about**
> AKS is cut and cloth — Eastern silhouettes held to a quieter line. Ready to wear, graded with care, finished properly. Modest by intention, not apology.

**Account — order history, empty**
> No orders yet. When you place one, it'll live here.

**Error — something failed**
> That didn't go through. Not your fault — try once more, and if it sulks again, message us on WhatsApp and we'll sort it.

**Footer — a single quiet line**
> AKS — عکس. Ready to wear. Cut and cloth.

## 6. The lexicon

**Words that are ours** (use freely): *ready to wear, cut and cloth, drape, finishing, size, karigar, aks / reflection, quiet, considered, the two worlds, yours.*

**Eastern words we use as-is:** *aks (reflection), karigar (the artisan), adda (the embroidery frame), jamawar / khaddar / lawn / organza (fabrics).*

**Words we never use** (see §3): luxury, elevated, curated, crafted with love, timeless, effortless, empower, journey, must-have, exclusive, premium, for the modern woman.

**Soft-launch: do not promise on the shop** (until a future ADR reopens MTM): *made to your measurements, give us your measure, nothing exists until you order it (as MTM), pay half now to begin cutting to your body.*

---

## 7. How this shows up everywhere (so it's not just words)

- **Ready to wear is the path.** Primary action: choose size → add to bag. No customer measurement form on soft launch ([ADR-0015](./decisions/ADR-0015-rtw-launch.md)).
- **Honest stock.** Sold-out sizes are real. Don't pretend every size is always available.
- **The chart is public.** `/size-guide` and PDP size & fit tell the truth in inches.
- **Track what you actually do.** Order status mirrors the production board — not a fictional MTM workshop play.
- **The look is the merger.** Bilingual wordmark, Eastern dyes in a calm layout. Nobody writes "East meets West" on a banner.

---

## 8. The single instruction, if you remember nothing else

> Say the true, specific thing plainly, warmly, in the second person — and let its meaning land on its own. Soft launch is ready-to-wear. Tell it like a friend who sews, and never like a brand.
